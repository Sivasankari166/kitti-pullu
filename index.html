How to Play

1

Flip the gilli. Tap, click, or press Space when the power bar looks good to flip the gilli up out of the trench.

2

Strike it mid-air. While it's up, a second timing bar appears — tap right near the white marker to connect.

3

Watch the flight. The camera pans to follow the gilli, a dotted trail traces its arc, and a live distance readout floats next to it so you always know where it's headed.

4

Solo Practice: 3 attempts per round, then level up — the strike window gets tighter each level.

5

Vs Computer: you and the computer each get 3 attempts. Longest single strike wins the match.

6

Pass & Play: same as Vs Computer, but you hand the device to Player 2 for their turn — they'll tap "Ready" before it starts.

7

Tip: you can also press the Space bar instead of tapping — it works for both the flip and the strike.
